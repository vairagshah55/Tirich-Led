TLC-123

Track-Mounted Spotlight

The TLC-123 is a compact track-mounted spotlight in black die-cast aluminium with a Shinland reflector and Bridgelux LEDs. It offers a 3-in-1 beam-angle change of 15°, 36° and 60° and delivers 130-140 LM/W. The 20W head mounts on a track rail for flexible, focused accent lighting.
